# Business requirements document — DRAFT derived from Master V1

## Business goal
Demonstrate an independently branded, controlled-access AI knowledge and Quality Engineering readiness assessment and its end-to-end engineering lifecycle. This is a professional development application, not an employer service.

## Stakeholders and personas
Participant with one of four assessment tracks (Manual QA, Automation QA, SDET, QA Lead); Administrator managing versioned assessment content and aggregate reporting. Track is not an authorization role.

## Business capabilities
BR-01 Participant can select a track, start/resume and submit one 20-question assessment.
BR-02 Platform shows reproducible, explainable results and approved learning recommendations.
BR-03 Admin governs original, research-backed, reviewable, publishable content with audit history.
BR-04 Access is controlled and participant records are private.
BR-05 Engineering team can test via Playwright UI/API and reproducible data.
BR-06 Product is independently branded, accessible and deployable to Render after approval.

## Explicit exclusions
Runtime LLM, enterprise SSO/HR integration, employee rankings, employer intellectual property, microservices, TaskPilot, Cursor, Selenium/Cucumber/Java automation.

## Dependencies and measures
Do not define industry readiness thresholds without approved evidence. Acceptance is measured against approved criteria, verified deterministic behavior, security boundaries, WCAG 2.2 AA target and executed tests; no implementation claims yet.
